Meet Sachin Nayyar

Founder, GuardianEye Inspections Inc.

My name is Sachin Nayyar, and I bring a unique blend of business leadership, construction-related quality control, and customer-focused service to the home inspection industry.

Before becoming a home inspector, I spent over 15 years in roles centered around quality assurance, operations, and risk-based decision-making across multiple industries. Most recently, I worked in manufacturing and production leadership at Durabuilt Windows & Doors, where I was directly involved in daily inspections, defect analysis, process improvement, and maintaining strict quality standards. This hands-on experience gave me a sharp eye for identifying issues that others might overlook—an essential skill in home inspections.

Throughout my career, I’ve been trusted to evaluate systems, analyze performance, and communicate findings clearly to both leadership teams and customers. Whether inspecting production lines, reviewing structural components, or identifying operational risks, my approach has always been the same: be thorough, be honest, and be precise.

As a home inspector, I apply that same disciplined mindset to every property I inspect. I understand that a home is often the largest investment a person will make, and my goal is to help clients make informed decisions with confidence. I don’t just look for problems—I explain what they mean, how serious they are, and what steps may be needed next, all in clear, easy-to-understand language.

My background in customer service, reporting, and stakeholder communication ensures that my inspection reports are detailed, well-organized, and practical— not filled with unnecessary jargon. I take the time to answer questions and walk clients through my findings, because I believe a great inspection is as much about education and transparency as it is about technical knowledge.

I am detail-oriented and committed to continuous learning in building systems, safety standards, and inspection best practices. Every inspection I perform is guided by integrity, professionalism, and respect for my clients’ peace of mind.

When you hire me, you’re not just getting a home inspector—you’re getting a professional who understands quality, accountability, and the importance of getting it right the first time.
